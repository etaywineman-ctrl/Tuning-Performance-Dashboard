Appendix: Demantra Evaluation Math Hardcoded Rules

Weighted WAPE

Ensures high-volume error impacts are penalized heavier than random low-value items:

WAPE = Σ|Act - Frcst| / ΣAct
Forecast Bias %

Indicates directional lean. Positive results reflect systematic over-buying excess risk:

Bias = Σ(Frcst - Act) / ΣAct
Relative FVA

Calculates raw improvement of our tuned engine parameters over the legacy Demantra state:

Impr % = (LegWAPE - TunWAPE) / LegWAPE
