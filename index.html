Calculation Methodology

Volume-Weighted WAPE

Calculates aggregate accuracy weighting high-volume items appropriately.

WAPE = Σ(|Actual - Forecast|) / Σ(Actual)
Aggregate Bias %

Measures systematic over-forecasting (+) or under-forecasting (-).

Bias % = Σ(Forecast - Actual) / Σ(Actual)
